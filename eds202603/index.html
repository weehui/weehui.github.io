Endless Match.Mahjong

A blend of mahjong tiles and classic match-puzzle stage play.

Design Philosophy

As a brand-new version in the Endless Match series, this time we bring the satisfying match-puzzle experience into the world of mahjong.

Familiar mahjong elements, richer stage variation, the strong thrill of chain clears, and gradually escalating strategic challenges together form the new Endless Match.Mahjong.

We hope Endless Match.Mahjong can continue the light and satisfying feel of the Endless Match series while bringing fresh fun unique to mahjong match gameplay.

Whether you play a few quick rounds in spare moments or seriously challenge difficult mahjong stages, we hope it gives you a relaxed, satisfying, and rewarding play session.

Gameplay Positioning

Familiar mahjong tiles are paired with easy-to-learn match gameplay. As stages progress, layouts and strategy become more challenging, while challenge modes, missions, and competitive leaderboards remain part of the experience.

Core Experience
  • 🧩 Thousands of Levels, Different Scenes: Each stage has different mahjong layouts and tile combinations, from relaxed casual play to high-difficulty challenges, with new puzzles waiting to be solved.
  • 💥 Satisfying Chain Clears: Match mahjong tiles in sequence to trigger chain clears and special effects.
  • 🔥 Nightmare Mahjong Stages, Experts Welcome: It is not just simple tapping. The real challenge is finding the best clearing order quickly, managing slots, and breaking through difficult layouts.
  • 🌍 Hundreds of Chinese-Style Themes: As you progress, you gradually unlock a wide range of Chinese-inspired scenes. Each theme has its own visual mood and stage design, making every run feel like traveling through a different Eastern world.
  • 🎁 Lucky Wheel, Free Daily Draws: Beyond mahjong match challenges, Mahjong adds a new lucky wheel. Log in daily for free draw chances, with coins, tools, reward boosts, and more. Whether you need resources or just want to see today's surprise, it adds more fun and anticipation to the journey.
